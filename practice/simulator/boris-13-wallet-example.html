Boris: One Wallet vs 13 Wallets

PRACTICE MODE — NO REAL MONEY. This example teaches staged entries, exits, reserve capital and risk awareness. It does not connect to wallets or execute trades.

Boris has a hypothetical 13 SOL practice budget. Compare putting it all into one entry with dividing exposure across 13 simulated wallets.

13 Simulated Wallets

Manipulation-awareness lesson: Many wallets do not automatically mean many independent traders. Coordinated timing, common funding patterns, concentrated supply, artificial-looking volume or a profitable public wallet accompanied by selling wallets can be warning signals. They are not proof by themselves. Never blindly copy-trade a wallet.
