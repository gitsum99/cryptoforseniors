13-Wallet Practice Simulator

PRACTICE ONLY — NO REAL MONEY.

Simple setup: 13 practice wallets. Each starts with 1 SOL. Total = 13 SOL.

This educational version uses the useful part of the Handsome Finance approach: study on-chain activity, spread entries instead of committing everything at once, take profits automatically at preset levels, and keep some wallets as reserve. It does not imitate market manipulation or artificial volume.

Practice price path: $10 → $8.50 → $11 → $9 → $12 → $7. Results are educational examples, not predictions.
